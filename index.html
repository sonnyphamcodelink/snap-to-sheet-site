Snap-to-Sheet

Turn document photos into spreadsheet rows. For iPhone with iOS 18 and later.

Photograph a receipt, a form or a printed table, and Snap-to-Sheet reads the values into the columns of your spreadsheet. Every row is shown for you to check and edit, and nothing is saved until you approve it.

Save to an Excel file on your iPhone, with no account needed, or to a Google Sheet in your Google Drive. If you don't have a file yet, start from a photo and the app reads the column names for you.
