Support

Snap-to-Sheet for iPhone

Contact

To report a problem or ask a question, open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iOS version, whether you save to Excel or Google Sheets, and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not attach photos of real documents.

Getting started

Choose where your rows go: an Excel file on your iPhone (from Files or iCloud Drive), a Google Sheet, or Start from a photo if you don't have a file yet. An existing file needs its column names in row 1. Take or pick up to ten photos, check the rows the app reads, then save.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Google account? Only for Google Sheets. Excel files on your iPhone need no account.

How many scans can I make? Free includes 30 scans a month on your iPhone. Plus includes 100 and Pro includes 500 scans a month, shared by Excel, Google Sheets and every Google account you use, on any device signed in with the same Apple ID. Each photo is one scan.

How do I restore my subscription on a new iPhone? Open Settings in the app, go to Plan, and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription? In the app, open Settings › Plan › Manage or cancel subscription, or go to iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

A save to Google Sheets said the result is unknown. What should I do? Open the sheet and check whether the rows are there before saving again, so they are not added twice. The app never retries an uncertain save on its own.

Can I undo a save to an Excel file? When the app replaces a file, it keeps a backup on your iPhone for seven days. Use Restore on the save receipt to put the original back.

How do I disconnect a Google account? Sign out of it in the app's Settings. You can also remove Snap-to-Sheet's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
